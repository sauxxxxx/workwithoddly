[Open the visual board](http://127.0.0.1:3213/output/storyboard/). Use Desktop / Mobile to compare the compositions. Every frame includes composition, transition, mobile, and asset notes.

## The page's story

**Meet the studio → understand the connection → inspect actual work → understand each discipline → browse the range → meet the studio's point of view → make contact.**

Noomo remains the reference for typography and sculpture occupying the same space. Lusion informs the scale, clarity, and pacing of project presentation. ODDLY supplies the actual palette, materials, geometry, typography, content, and project work.

## Shared direction and behavior

- Cream remains continuous from the opening through the work and capability chapters. Charcoal marks the final invitation. Color changes have a compositional purpose.
- Keep the existing condensed display face, charcoal type, coral punctuation, and clay material family. Sentence copy uses the established body/brand faces.
- Navigation exposes Work, Services, About, and Contact. Use compact transparent controls rather than a full-width opaque strip cutting across the scenes. Maintain safe space behind the controls in resting frames and sufficient contrast on every surface.
- Every large transition has one lead: object, type, or media. Supporting elements follow. Avoid several unrelated effects competing at once.
- Each scene must look composed at rest and while entering/leaving. Check intermediate frames, reverse scrolling, and anchor jumps as well as the final pose.
- No essential words, services, links, or project information depend on hover, WebGL, a recording, or motion.
- Reduced motion preserves the resting frames and pauses automatic films. Unavailable WebGL uses authored stills. Media controls remain available.
- Defer offscreen media. Mobile may use stills and fewer live effects while retaining the composition. A physical-device performance review belongs to implementation.
