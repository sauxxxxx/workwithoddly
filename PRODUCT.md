# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, static export, Vercel-ready) + React Three Fiber / Drei for real-time 3D + GSAP ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography. Chosen by the user from a recommended option (2026-10-15). No prior codebase existed; the previous deployment (oddly-eight.vercel.app) returns DEPLOYMENT_NOT_FOUND.

## Users

Business owners and decision-makers (hospitality, professional services, product founders) evaluating a partner to build digital products, automate operations, or produce marketing. They arrive from referrals or search, skim for evidence of capability across disciplines, then contact the studio.

## Product Purpose

ODDLY® is a Creative Technology Studio in Cebu, Philippines. It brings together technology, automation, and creative services so businesses can build digital products, improve operations, and communicate more effectively. The website must make all three disciplines legible, show real work, and lead to contact.

## Positioning

One studio that ships the website, the internal system behind it (CRM, accounting, automation, AI), and the campaign that markets it. Brand statement: "Different by design. Built for better."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Technology: website development, application development, custom software, UI/UX design, digital products and business systems.
- Automation: AI solutions, business workflow automation, CRM systems, internal business tools, AI-powered products and integrations.
- Creative: digital marketing, SEO, content creation, video editing, social media graphics, campaign design and creative production.
- Navigation must expose Work, Services, About, Contact directly.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work and the range — technology, automation, and creative carry equal weight.
2. Never invent proof; mark every placeholder as replaceable.
3. Expression serves comprehension: every visitor can reach any service, project, or contact in one move.
4. Performance and accessibility are part of the craft, not a later pass.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard navigation with visible focus, prefers-reduced-motion respected, content usable when WebGL is unavailable.
